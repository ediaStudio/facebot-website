Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

FaceBot is operated by DHA Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the FaceBot mobile app for iOS and Android, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the app and to this website.

Questions about this policy or your data: contact@dhastudio.xyz.

1. Information we collect

  • Photos and videos you choose. When you select a photo or video for an age estimate, a skin analysis, or an AI transformation, we upload that file to our servers. It may contain your face and other people's faces.
  • Generated results. The images and videos created by the app, and the information attached to them (estimated age range, gender, ethnicity keywords, background keywords, face position, skin attributes).
  • Account information. A user ID, your sign-in email address if you sign in with Google, your device identifier, push notification token, language, and basic device and app information.
  • Purchases and subscriptions. Which plan or credit pack you bought, the transaction identifier, and the subscription status. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process the payment; we never receive your card number.
  • Usage and diagnostics. Screens used, actions taken, crash reports, and performance data. If ads are shown, our advertising partner may receive an advertising identifier.

We do not require your name, postal address, or phone number to use the app.

2. How we use information

  • To produce the photo or video result you requested.
  • To estimate an apparent age range and to analyse skin attributes when you use those features.
  • To keep your history, manage credits and subscriptions, and provide customer support.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the app, and to detect abuse or fraud.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your photos or face data for advertising, and we do not use them to identify you.

3. Face data

This section answers specifically how FaceBot handles face data. It applies when a photo you submit contains a face, whether or not the feature is described as an age or skin feature.

3.1 What face data we collect

We collect the photo you choose to submit, which may include your face, and the result generated from it. From that photo, our service providers return an estimated age range, gender, ethnicity keywords, background keywords, the position of the face in the image, and, for the optional skin analysis, skin attributes. We store those results with your history entry.

We do not create or store a face template, faceprint, face embedding, facial geometry map, or any other biometric identifier. We do not use face data to recognise or identify a person, and we do not compare one user's face with another person's face.

3.2 How we use face data

We use the photo only to provide the feature you requested: estimating an apparent age, producing a skin analysis, or generating the AI photo or video you selected. The result is shown to you and saved in your history so you can view or delete it. We do not use face data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the feature you requested.

3.3 Who processes face data and where it is stored

  • Google Cloud / Firebase (Google LLC) hosts our backend and stores the photos and results in its EU multi-region storage. This is where the files are stored.
  • Everypixel receives a signed, read-only URL to the photo and returns the estimated age, gender, ethnicity, and background keywords.
  • Face++ (Megvii) receives the photo only if you use the optional skin analysis feature, through Face++'s US API endpoint, and returns skin attributes.
  • fal.ai, Runware, and OpenAI receive the photo (or a signed URL to it) and your selected template or prompt only when you request an AI generation that uses them.

These providers process the image to return the requested result. They are not authorised by us to use your face data for their own advertising or to sell it. Their own privacy policies also apply: Google, Everypixel, Face++, fal.ai, Runware, and OpenAI.

We do not sell face data, share it with data brokers, or disclose it to advertisers.

3.4 How long we keep face data

  • Uploaded photos and generated media stored with your account are automatically deleted 180 days after they are uploaded.
  • Photos uploaded for automated photo-quality checks or the optional skin analysis are stored separately and deleted when you delete the result or ask us to delete it; we process deletion requests within 30 days.
  • Analysis results and history entries (age range, keywords, skin attributes) are kept until you delete the entry in the app or ask us to erase them.

3.5 How to delete face data

  • Delete an individual photo, video, or analysis from your History in the app.
  • Delete your account from the app's settings. This removes your account and sign-in credentials; you can also email us to have your remaining history and stored files erased.
  • Email contact@dhastudio.xyz from the address linked to your account. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

3.6 What we never do with face data

  • No facial recognition or identity verification.
  • No faceprints, templates, or biometric identifiers.
  • No sale or rental of face data.
  • No use of face data to target advertising.
  • No sharing with other users or with the public.

4. Other third-party services

We use the following services to operate FaceBot. They receive only the information needed for their function.

  • Google Cloud / Firebase — hosting, database, storage, and analytics.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play — payment processing and app distribution.
  • Google AdMob — advertising, where ads are shown. AdMob may receive an advertising identifier; it does not receive your photos or face data.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above:

  • Photos and generated media stored with your account: automatically deleted after 180 days.
  • Photo-quality and skin analysis images: until you delete them or ask us to.
  • History entries and analysis results: until you delete them or ask us to erase them.
  • Account and purchase records: while your account is active, then as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Diagnostics and crash data: kept for a limited period, then deleted or aggregated.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Access to stored photos is limited to the systems and staff that need it to operate the service and to handle support or deletion requests.

7. Children

FaceBot is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, email contact@dhastudio.xyz. We respond within 30 days.

9. International transfers

Your photos and results are stored in the European Union (Google Cloud EU multi-region). Some of our service providers process data in the United States, as described in Section 3.3. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

How to Remove and Replace Photo Backgrounds with AI

August 27, 2026 · 4 min read

Removing a background used to mean opening a desktop editor, zooming in, and carefully tracing around every strand of hair. FaceBot does it in seconds with AI, and the cutout is usually clean enough that nobody can tell the background was never there.

What you can do with a background removal

The most common uses are practical: a clean profile picture for work, a product photo for a listing, or a portrait with a plain white background for an official document. Then there are the fun uses: dropping yourself into a famous landmark, a beach, or a completely imaginary scene.

Because the tool separates the person from the background, you can place them anywhere. That is the same principle behind many of the most shared AI photos on social media.

How to do it in FaceBot

Open FaceBot and choose the background tool. Select the photo, and the AI detects the person automatically. You can remove the background completely to get a transparent cutout, or replace it with a new one from the built-in collection.

If you want to use your own background, pick a plain image from your gallery and the app will place the cutout over it. You can also adjust the position and size of the person before saving.

Choosing the right source photo

Background removal works best when the person stands out from the background. A clear contrast between the subject and what is behind them gives the cleanest edge. For example, a person in a dark jacket against a light wall cuts out perfectly.

Photos with busy backgrounds, like crowds or foliage, are harder. The AI still handles them, but the edge around the person may need a closer look. When in doubt, take a new photo in front of a plain wall.

Lighting matters too. Soft, even light on the face and shoulders produces a much cleaner cutout than harsh sunlight or heavy shadows.

Hair, glasses, and other tricky details

Hair is the hardest part of any background removal, which is why the old manual tools took so long. AI handles flyaway hairs surprisingly well, but very frizzy hair against a busy background can still cause small imperfections. A quick fix is to zoom in on the result and, if needed, run the photo through the AI enhancer afterward to clean up the edges.

Glasses are usually fine, as long as they do not have heavy reflections. Hats and scarves are no problem at all.

What to do with your cutouts

Build a consistent set of profile pictures for every platform. Create a simple product-style photo for a side hustle listing. Or combine cutouts with other transformations: put your cutout on a background and then run an AI selfie style to merge everything into one cohesive scene.

Background replacement also pairs naturally with the photo to video tool, since a clean subject makes the animated result much more stable.

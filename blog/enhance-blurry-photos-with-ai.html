How to Enhance Blurry Photos with AI

August 26, 2026 · 5 min read

Everyone has that one photo that matters. A family moment, a travel shot, a portrait from an old phone. And almost always, the photo is slightly blurry, a bit dark, or too low-resolution to use anywhere. The FaceBot AI enhancer fixes exactly this kind of image, and the improvement is often bigger than people expect.

What the AI enhancer does

The enhancer rebuilds your image at a higher quality. It sharpens edges, reduces noise, smooths out compression artifacts, and brings back detail in dark areas. On a portrait, the effect is most visible on the face: eyes become sharper, skin texture looks cleaner, and the whole photo gains a crispness that was not there before.

It also upscales the resolution. A small image from an old phone or a screenshot can be enlarged to a size suitable for printing or posting, without turning into a pixelated mess.

What it cannot do

Being honest here saves you time. The AI cannot invent detail that does not exist in the photo. If a face is completely out of focus, with no features visible at all, no tool will bring the eyes back. It works by recovering and sharpening existing information, not by creating a new photo from scratch.

Extreme cases also have limits: a photo that is 100 by 100 pixels will never become a magazine cover. But for the common cases, a slightly soft photo, a low-res download, an old scanned picture, the result is usually impressive.

How to enhance a photo in FaceBot

Open FaceBot and select the enhance or HD option on the home screen. Pick the photo from your gallery, let the app process it, and compare the result with the original side by side. If you like what you see, save the enhanced version. It is a single step, and it takes only a few seconds.

Tips for the best results

Start from the largest version you have. Do not enhance a screenshot of a photo if you still have the original file somewhere. More source information means a better result.

Faces respond best. The enhancer is strongest on portraits because the AI knows exactly what a face should look like. Landscape shots improve too, but the gain is most obvious on people.

Enhance before you transform. If you plan to run another transformation, like making an AI video or removing the background from a photo, enhance the image first. Every AI tool works better on a clean, sharp input.

Keep the original. Save the enhanced photo as a new file and keep the original. You never know when you will want to go back to it.

When to use the enhancer

Use it before printing an old family photo, before posting a low-quality picture on social media, or before using an image in a design. Use it also as a preparation step for other AI transformations, since every effect in FaceBot benefits from a sharp source image.

If your photo is blurry because it was taken in bad light, the enhancer will recover what it can, but nothing beats taking a new picture in good light. Combine both approaches: take the best photo you can, then let the AI polish it.

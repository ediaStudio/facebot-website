10 AI Selfie Styles You Should Try

August 26, 2026 · 6 min read

AI selfies are the easiest way to see yourself in a version of the world that never existed. One photo, one tap, and you are a movie character, an anime hero, or a renaissance portrait. FaceBot has a wide library of styles, and these ten are the ones people keep coming back to.

Before diving in, one rule applies to every style: use a clear, frontal, well-lit photo with one face. The better the input, the more convincing the transformation.

1. Cinematic portrait

The classic that started it all. A dramatic, movie-poster look with rich colors, soft lighting, and a background that looks like a film set. This is the style to use for a profile picture that gets compliments.

2. Anime character

Your face redrawn in a clean anime style, with big expressive eyes and stylized hair. A favorite among younger users, and instantly recognizable when posted.

3. Fantasy warrior

Armor, dramatic lighting, and a world that looks straight out of an epic film. The fantasy style works best with a neutral expression, the AI takes care of the rest.

4. Professional headshot

A polished, studio-quality portrait with a neutral background and flattering light. Ideal for LinkedIn, resumes, or any place where a sharp professional photo matters.

5. Renaissance painting

You as a classical oil painting, complete with period clothing and old-master lighting. It looks surprisingly natural and makes a great conversation piece.

6. Cyberpunk city

Neon lights, rain-slicked streets, and a futuristic vibe. The cyberpunk style is bold and colorful, perfect for anyone who wants something visually loud.

7. Vintage film star

A retro Hollywood look with soft focus and classic glamour. This style softens the face and adds warmth, giving photos an elegant, timeless feel.

8. Superhero

You in full hero mode, with dramatic lighting and a costume that fits your photo. Great for kids, great for adults, and even better in a group challenge.

9. Winter wonderland

Snow, warm clothes, and cozy lighting. A seasonal favorite that works year-round because the result is simply beautiful.

10. Abstract art

Your face reinterpreted through bold colors and modern art styles. Less realistic, more artistic, and perfect when you want something unusual for an avatar.

How to get the most out of each style

Generate a few variations of the same style and pick the best one. The AI does not always nail it on the first attempt, and a different expression in your source photo can completely change the mood of the result.

Try pairing styles with other tools. Run the age filter first and then apply a style, so you can see a cinematic version of your future self. Or enhance a low-quality photo with the AI enhancer before transforming it, sharp inputs always give sharper styles.

Where to share your AI selfies

Profile pictures on social networks, WhatsApp statuses, group chat reactions, and short-form video posts are the natural homes for AI selfies. One small note: if you are transforming photos of other people, ask for permission before posting. It keeps the fun safe and respectful.
